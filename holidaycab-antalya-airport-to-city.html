How to get from Antalya Airport to Antalya

HC
HolidayCab EditorialTravel team · Updated June 2026
AYT · 15 km from city centre · 5 transport options compared

On this page

Antalya Airport sits around 15 km east of the city center, and there are five options available when traveling between the two. You can take your pick between a private airport transfer, an Antalya Airport taxi, tram, shuttle bus, or local bus. Getting a HolidayCab airport transfer is the most comfortable and timely option, as you'll be assured of door-to-door service no matter what time you arrive.

Taking the tram is also convenient and significantly cheaper, running frequently from Antalya Airport to the city center. While the tram is great if you land during the day, it's not viable for anyone arriving in the early hours or late into the night.

Another alternative is to catch the airport shuttle or the public bus. These are among the most budget-friendly ways to travel, and though departures are regular, you may face a wait depending on when you arrive.

🚕How to get from Antalya Airport to Antalya city by taxi

Getting a taxi from Antalya Airport to the city center is a convenient and comfortable way to travel. When you opt for an Antalya Airport taxi, you'll be dropped at the door of your hotel without figuring out public transport timetables or dragging your luggage around.

Taxis serve the airport 24 hours a day, so you won't worry about getting a ride even if you arrive late. On average, the drive takes about 25 minutes.

Cost of a taxi from Antalya Airport to the city

The cost depends on the distance to your specific destination, as cabs are metered. On average, the ride totals around €12.30 (TRY 461), regardless of the time of day. That said, many drivers offer set rates that can be significantly higher — it's best to only travel with drivers who agree to turn on the meter.

Where do I get a taxi from?

All three terminals at Antalya Airport have a designated taxi rank right outside the Arrivals Hall. Follow the transport signs out of your terminal and join the queue at the rank outside.

Convenience

Taking a taxi is typically a quick, hassle-free way to get around. Because they service the airport day and night, you'll have no problem getting a ride. Queues can form if many flights land at once, so you may sometimes face a wait.

Useful tips
  • Although some taxis accept card, ask your driver before starting the trip.
  • Always ensure the meter is reset at the beginning of a ride to avoid overcharging.
  • Only accept a ride with drivers who agree to use the meter rather than a fixed fare.
  • Use a maps app to preview the most efficient route; some drivers take longer alternatives.

🚊How to get from Antalya Airport to the city center by tram

Taking the tram from Antalya Airport to the city center is quick and efficient. The AntRay line passes through the airport roughly every 15 minutes and stops at central spots like 'İsmet Paşa'. The journey takes around 30 minutes, depending on stops and time of day.

How much is the tram from Antalya Airport to the city center?

You have two ticket options. The first is a single-use card, which includes the journey (€0.50 / TRY 20) plus a one-time card (€0.30 / TRY 10). Alternatively, an Antalyakart top-up card costs €1.20 (TRY 45), with the standard €0.50 fare still applying. Tickets are bought at the automated machines at the tram station.

Cost€0.80
Trip duration30 Min.

Average wait of 10–20 minutes for the AntRay line.

Convenience ★★★★★
Safety ★★★★★
Value for money ★★★★★
iThe AntRay tram operates from 06:00 to 00:00.

Where do I get the tram from?

Havalimanı tram station is a short walk from both terminals. From the Arrivals Hall in any terminal, follow the signage to the tram.

Convenience

For the most part, the tram is fast, inexpensive, and reliable. Trams depart frequently and are easy to navigate, but can be crowded during peak hours and uncomfortable with a lot of luggage.

Useful tips
  • Check how far your hotel is from the nearest tram stop to get off at the best stop.
  • If you buy an Antalyakart, top it up quickly using the Antalyakart Mobil app.
  • Payment must be made by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.
  • The tram is the fastest public-transport option, avoiding most traffic.

🚌How to get from Antalya Airport to the city center by shuttle bus

Another popular option among budget-conscious travelers is the shuttle bus. Operated by Havas, these shuttles take around 45 minutes to reach the city center and stop in various parts of the city, including the Bus Terminal.

However, these shuttles depart after each domestic flight lands, so the timetable is sometimes unpredictable and may be inconvenient for international arrivals.

How much is the shuttle bus from Antalya Airport to the city center?

Although prices vary depending on your stop, the shuttle from Antalya Airport to the city is approximately €3.30 (TRY 125).

Cost€3.30
Trip duration45 Min.

Waiting times can be upwards of an hour, as shuttles depart after domestic flights arrive.

Where do I get the shuttle bus from?

The Havas Airport Shuttle departs from right outside Terminal 3, which handles domestic flights.

Convenience

While convenient for anyone arriving at the domestic terminal, it's not the most suitable for passengers landing elsewhere. Shuttles depart roughly hourly but depend on arrival times, leading to lengthy waits.

Useful tips

  • The Havas shuttles are usually coaches, more comfortable than local city buses.
  • Shuttles run hourly during the day but are limited at night.
  • There's a short walk from Terminals 1 and 2 to Terminal 3, where the shuttle leaves.

🚐How to get from Antalya Airport to the city center by bus

The fifth and final way to travel from Antalya Airport to the city is by local bus. During the day, the 400, 600, and 800 buses depart from the airport, reaching the city center and stopping at central spots like 'Yener Ulusoy' within 45 minutes in light traffic. At night, the 600G bus operates instead, though departures are more infrequent.

How much is the bus from Antalya Airport to the city center?

A one-way ticket on a local bus from Antalya Airport will set you back about €1.10 (TRY 40) per person. Tickets are available directly from the driver and can generally be paid for using cash or card.

Cost€1.10
Trip duration45 Min.

Local buses depart roughly every hour.

Where do I get the bus from?

There are bus stops close to each of the three terminals. Some are situated between Terminals 1 and 3, while others are directly outside Terminal 2.

Convenience

Catching the local bus is affordable and departures are relatively regular, but waiting times can still be up to an hour — particularly late at night. These buses can be crowded during peak times.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Antalya Airport to the city center?

Taxi costs from Antalya Airport to Antalya can vary substantially. Although taxis are metered, many drivers offer a fixed fare in advance. With a metered taxi, expect the drive to cost around €12.30 (TRY 463) on average. With HolidayCab, you'll pay €23 (TRY 865) for this trip while eliminating waiting times and the need to negotiate.

How do I get to Antalya city center from the airport?

You have five options: a private HolidayCab transfer (door-to-door, 25 min), a metered taxi, the AntRay tram, the Havas shuttle bus, or a local bus (400/600/800). A private transfer is the most comfortable; the tram is the cheapest daytime option.

How far is Antalya city center from the airport?

Antalya Airport is around 15 km east of the city center. The drive takes about 25 minutes in normal traffic.

How much is a bus ticket from Antalya Airport to the city center?

A one-way local bus ticket costs about €1.10 (TRY 40) per person, payable to the driver by cash or card. The Havas shuttle is approximately €3.30 (TRY 125).

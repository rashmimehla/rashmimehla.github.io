How to get from Antalya Airport to Side

HolidayCab Editorial
Travel team · Updated June 2025

Set along Turkey's Mediterranean coast is the historical town of Side. Once a prominent port, Side now draws visitors from around the world with its impressive ancient ruins and relaxed seaside charm. Located just 65 kilometers east of Antalya International Airport (AYT), Side offers convenient connections for both domestic and international travelers.

When traveling from Antalya Airport to Side, there are three main transport options: a taxi, shared shuttle bus, or a private airport transfer. For a simple and easy way to reach Side, taking an Antalya Airport taxi is a reliable option — taxis are readily available and the journey takes around one hour. If you prefer to avoid negotiating fares or handling local currency, a HolidayCab airport transfer is the superior choice. Booking a private transfer costs just €41 and ensures you'll be greeted at arrivals by a professional, English-speaking driver and taken directly to your destination in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

While there are no direct public buses operating from Antalya Airport to Side, budget-conscious travelers can opt to take a shared airport shuttle. These typically take around 1 hour and 45 minutes, not including potential waiting times, and may involve several stops before reaching your destination.

How to take a taxi from Antalya Airport to Side

If you're looking for a straightforward and hassle-free journey, taking a taxi from Antalya Airport to Side is one of the most efficient modes of transport. An Antalya Airport taxi requires no advance booking and offers a direct, door-to-door service — particularly valuable for travelers with luggage, small children, or those arriving late at night. During peak tourist seasons there may be a short wait at the taxi stand.

How much does a taxi cost from Antalya Airport to Side?

Official taxis at Antalya Airport are fitted with meters. Covering a distance of 65 kilometers, the journey takes 1 hour and costs approximately €65 (₺2,900 TL) in normal traffic. Prices generally remain consistent without nighttime or holiday surcharges, but may rise in heavy traffic. Most taxis accept cash and card — if paying by card, confirm with the driver in advance.

Where can I take a taxi from?

The taxi stands at Antalya Airport are located directly outside the Arrival Halls of all three terminals. They are easily identifiable by their bright yellow colour and their yellow and black "Taksi" signs on the roof.

Convenience

★★★★★

Taking a taxi is one of the most convenient options when traveling from Antalya Airport to Side. Although it may be more expensive than a shared shuttle, it offers a much faster and more direct journey, with no stops along the way.

Editor's Note ▼

Tipping in Turkey isn't mandatory, but it's always appreciated. Like in many countries, both locals and travelers often round up the taxi fare as a simple gesture of thanks.

Useful Tips ▼
  • ✓ Ensure the taximeter has been reset and turned on before starting your trip.
  • ✓ Have your hotel address written down or available on your phone.
  • ✓ Carry some cash in small notes in case your driver doesn't have change.
  • ✓ Always take a taxi from the official taxi ranks at the airport.

How to get from Antalya Airport to Side by shared shuttle bus

There is no direct bus service from Antalya Airport to Side. Travelers seeking a cheaper option can take a shared shuttle bus, which waits at the airport until all booked passengers have arrived before departing for Side. Upon reaching the town, the shuttle makes multiple stops to drop passengers at their hotels. The entire journey usually takes about 1 hour and 45 minutes, depending on traffic and stops.

How much is the shared shuttle bus from Antalya Airport to Side?

The cost can vary depending on how far in advance you book. Generally, a one-way ticket costs approximately €12, making this the most affordable option. The price includes a luggage allowance of one suitcase per passenger; check the provider's baggage policy if you have more.

Where do I catch the shared shuttle bus from?

After collecting your luggage, make your way to the Arrivals Hall, where a travel representative from your booked company will meet you. Meeting-point details are in your confirmation email. They'll guide you to your vehicle and let you know how many more passengers you'll wait for.

Convenience

★★★★★

Taking a shared shuttle bus is less convenient than other options. In addition to possible wait times for passengers on different flights, the shuttle will likely make multiple stops. Unless you're the first dropped off, this can add a lot of time. For travelers prioritizing cost over convenience, it remains a practical choice.

Editor's Note ▼

After booking your shared transfer, you'll receive an email confirmation. Keep this handy, as you'll need to show it to your driver or travel representative when boarding.

Useful Tips ▼
  • ✓ You must reserve your seat in the shared shuttle online, beforehand.
  • ✓ Traveling as a couple or group? A taxi or private transfer is more cost-savvy.
  • ✓ The shuttle makes several hotel stops in Side, which can extend travel time.
  • ✓ Allow extra time during peak travel to accommodate delays.
